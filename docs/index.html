Use case · Telecom customer support

An AI support agent that can safely touch a telco's billing, network and SIMs.

Most RAG demos answer questions. A telecom support agent also takes payments, changes plans and touches SIMs, under fraud, regulation and outages. This platform shows how to build one where agents and models propose and a policy-enforcing tool gateway decides. All back-end systems are labelled sandbox mocks.

The problem

Why telecom support is a hard agent problem

Five real-world constraints shaped the design. A generic "LLM plus vector database" stack fails on each of them.

CPNI / PII regulation

Sensitive data leaks into prompts and logs

Card numbers, PINs and phone numbers end up in model prompts, logs and handoff notes.

Fix: a scrubber tokenizes PII before anything reaches a model and restores it only at delivery.

SIM-swap fraud

An empathetic model gets socially engineered

"I'm stranded and lost my phone" is exactly what an attacker says to get an eSIM.

Fix: SIM and eSIM changes are blocked in policy code unless out-of-band MFA is verified.

Outage storms

One fiber cut becomes thousands of chats

Identical "my internet is dead" conversations exhaust GPUs and back-end connection pools.

Fix: a geofence deflects subscribers on affected towers with a broadcast message.

Grandfathered contracts

Search surfaces the wrong plan

Vector search shows the 2026 plan's 50 GB hotspot to a 2018 customer who contractually has 10 GB.

Fix: retrieval is filtered by the subscriber's activation date before ranking.

Legacy OSS/BSS

Slow back ends invite hallucination

A billing database that takes 15 seconds to answer hangs the agent or tempts it to guess.

Fix: circuit breakers isolate failing tools and hand off to a human.

How it works

One request, eight stages

The probabilistic parts propose. Deterministic code disposes. Anything that moves money, changes a SIM or touches CPNI is gated by policy an LLM can't talk its way past.

  1. PII / CPNI scrubberRegex and NER replace identifiers with salted tokens before any model sees the text.
  2. Inference routerPredictable or PII-heavy work goes to on-prem vLLM (Llama 3.1 70B, zero marginal token cost). Complex multi-turn or multimodal work bursts to Gemini on GCP.
  3. Classical ML triageMulti-intent classifier, frustration velocity (sentiment slope per turn), churn-risk score, and dialect / code-switching normalizer for noisy ASR.
  4. 4-tier hybrid RAGDense vectors, BM25 for hex codes and model numbers, GraphRAG over network topology, then reciprocal-rank fusion and reranking, with strict temporal filtering.
  5. Hierarchical multi-agent DAGA supervisor routes to Customer Care, Billing, Sales, Network, SIM & Device, Order, Retention and Knowledge agents over shared state, with a step budget and no delegation loops.
  6. OSS / BSS tool adaptersEvery system call goes through one tool gateway: typed arguments, ownership check, policy, single-use confirmation, step-up, human approval, idempotency, timeouts and a circuit breaker. Adapters are sandbox mocks.
  7. Deterministic guardrailsGrounding and factuality checks, regulatory filters and CPNI-leak prevention on every draft answer.
  8. Delivery or warm handoffSafe, grounded answers go to the customer with tokens restored. Anything else goes to a Tier-2 human with a pre-filled dossier.
Scenarios

See how it handles real cases

Four edge cases the platform is built and tested for. Pick one. The conversations are illustrative paraphrases, not recorded output.

Verified customer disputes a roaming charge

I was charged for roaming on a trip I never took. Can you fix this?
I can see the roaming line on your invoice and the call records behind it. I can't issue a credit myself, so I've registered a dispute with that evidence for a billing specialist. Shall I confirm?
  • Scrub: the account number and phone number are tokenized.
  • Triage: billing dispute intent, with a secondary churn signal.
  • Plan: the supervisor routes to the Billing agent.
  • Act: the agent inspects the invoice, call records and tariff, registers the dispute after consent and confirmation, and routes it to a specialist. Credits are never autonomous: the policy pack sets the autonomous limit to 0.
  • Guardrail: the answer is checked against the retrieved invoice facts.
Outcome: evidence-backed dispute registered, a human decides any credit, every step audited.

Someone asks for a new eSIM with no identity proof

I lost my phone and I'm stranded. Please move my number to a new eSIM right now.
I can't do that until we verify it's you. I've sent a verification request, and I can connect you to a specialist.
  • Triage: urgency and emotion detected. This is the classic pressure pattern.
  • Identity check: the session's assurance level is below out-of-band MFA.
  • Policy: the SIM & Device agent's eSIM action needs OTP step-up and human approval, enforced in code, not by a prompt instruction.
  • Handoff: a Tier-2 dossier records the request, the block and the reason.
Outcome: the sympathetic story doesn't change the result. No SIM change without verified MFA.

A fiber cut takes down a whole sector

My internet has been dead for an hour. Please help!
We're aware of a fiber cut affecting your area (Sector 7). Engineers are working on it, and we'll message you when service is restored.
  • Locate: the subscriber is attached to a tower with an active critical alarm.
  • Geofence: routine inquiries are deflected with a broadcast message before any expensive reasoning.
  • Protect: GPU capacity and human queues are saved for cases that actually need them.
Outcome: thousands of duplicate chats are answered with one cheap, accurate message.

Two customers ask about hotspot data

How much hotspot data do I get each month?
On your 2018 plan, 10 GB. (A customer activated in 2026 would hear 50 GB.)
  • Retrieve: dense, sparse and graph search return candidate passages.
  • Filter: the temporal filter keeps only documents valid on the subscriber's activation date.
  • Answer: the response cites the contract the customer actually has.
Outcome: no accidental promise of a benefit the customer doesn't have.
Capabilities

What's in the platform

Cloud

Hybrid cloud arbitrage

A simulated router models on-prem versus cloud cost and latency trade-offs. It makes no live vLLM or Vertex calls.

Retrieval

4-tier hybrid RAG

Dense, BM25, GraphRAG and fusion, filtered by contract validity dates.

Agents

Supervisor plus eight specialists

Customer Care, Billing, Sales, Network, SIM & Device, Order, Retention and Knowledge agents with least-privilege permissions and bounded autonomy.

Safety

Zero-trust by default

Deny-by-default policy pack, signed session tokens, step-up verification, hash-chained audit, admin API keys, circuit breakers and a grounding guardrail.

Humans

Tier-2 warm handoff

Summary, priority, suggested next action and full transcript; operators claim, reply and resolve while the customer sees the handover.

MLOps

Drift and self-healing

PSI and K-S drift monitors, canary model registry, and one-click retuning of RAG weights.

Metrics

The Measured KPIs tab and /api/v1/admin/kpis compute plan KPIs from audited events and return null when there is no data. The six-category table below describes the older illustrative board, whose values are demo data.

Command Center dashboard

A web console for support supervisors, NOC engineers and AI ops: an operator chat console with a live reasoning trace, a network topology and alarm view, cost arbitrage counters, drift monitors, the illustrative six-category board, measured KPIs, approvals and the human handover queue. A separate customer chat page lives at /customer.

Honest scope: this is a reference implementation on sandbox adapters. No operator system is connected, replies are built deterministically from tool results, there is no live LLM call (an interface with a mock default exists), embeddings are TF-IDF and the reranker is lexical. State is in memory, login is a sandbox PIN, and speech, rate limiting and persistence are not built. The orchestration, policy, confirmation, step-up and audit logic are real and tested. cd BSP-CSC-RAG-Agent
